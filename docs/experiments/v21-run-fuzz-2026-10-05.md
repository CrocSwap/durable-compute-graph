# v2.1 run-level dispute fuzzer (2026-10-05)

**Status: RESULT_STATUS.** Alpha plan R1, exit criterion 2: "the run-level
dispute fuzzer: several disputes per run, random interleavings, invariants
checked after every step; it runs clean, natively and on SBF."

## Harness

`crates/dcg-program/tests/disputes_v21_run_fuzz.rs` (feature `graph-v21`).
As in the sessions v3 fuzzer, the generator and the reference model are in
Rust next to ProgramTest. Sequence `i` of seed `s` depends only on `(s, i)`
and the program's answers.

**Each sequence** is one template on the Hello Graph goldens:
- random windows (challenge 750–2,500 slots, phase 750–1,200), bonds, and
  slasher share (1–9,999 bps);
- a committed truth: honest (30%), or any mix of a wrong step output, an
  absent leaf and a wrong posted output;
- a diligent or lazy executor;
- 30% of sequences also have a second run that is never committed (it is
  cancelled at the end);
- one to seven disputes:
  - honest challengers, who are protected: they always move on time;
  - griefers, who make any move, at any time, or none; some reopen at a
    closed dispute's address;
  - executor puppets, where the executor challenges its own lie.

**Actions,** interleaved at random:
- the owed moves, directly or through staging buffers (create, grow by
  either party or the payer, write), and cache answers;
- clock warps, and probes at a deadline's exact slot and the slot after (a
  move at the deadline is accepted and a timeout refused; one slot later the
  reverse), and the same for the challenge window's last slot;
- every settlement call (timeout, moot, advance, pay_pot, finalize, and the
  closes of disputes, caches, runs and templates), plus retire, and an
  `init_run` on a retired template;
- perturbations that must be refused:
  - a wrong signer;
  - an out-of-turn move;
  - a corrupted reveal (a flipped bit, a truncation, an extra byte);
  - a wrong recipient on a call the model would otherwise accept;
  - a staging write by the party that does not own the buffer;
  - a forged copy of a live dispute.

**Checked after every transaction:**
- **Shadow ledger.** Every tracked account's balance must equal the model's
  prediction for that instruction. The model covers:
  - creation rent, the executor bond at commit, the challenger bond at open;
  - the bond move at each ruling (to the executor if it wins, otherwise back
    to the challenger) and at each moot;
  - the pot split (the slasher share to the winner, the rest to the payer),
    and the bond returned at finalize;
  - each close's refunds: the other party's recorded growth, the creator's
    rent, the dispute's rent to its challenger, the run down to its receipt
    floor, the cache to its executor, and the template to its payer.

  A refused instruction must change nothing.
- **Precondition model.** Every settlement call with the right accounts must
  be accepted exactly when the program's rules allow it, and refused
  otherwise.
- **Run and dispute state:**
  - the run status moves only COMMITTED to FINAL or REFUTED;
  - the run's open-dispute and executor-wait counters match the live
    disputes;
  - its sequence and closed counters match the accepted opens and closes;
  - a ruling never changes;
  - `best` is the lowest sequence ruled for the challenger;
  - the pot is paid only after the ruled prefix passes `best`;
  - the receipt holds exactly its rent floor.
- **Ruling oracles:**
  - SHAPE: an absent leaf is convicted, a well-formed one is not;
  - OUT: wrong exactly when the posted output differs from the second
    step's output;
  - STEP at the second step: wrong exactly when its output is;
  - a timeout rules against the party that owed the move;
  - anything opened after the lowest challenger win is ruled moot (review
    10-03, F1);
  - an honest commitment answered diligently is never ruled for the
    challenger, and an honest challenger is never ruled against.
- The bystander's balance never changes.

**At the end,** every account closes. Honest parties must net non-negative.
A lie disputed by an honest challenger must end REFUTED, and an honest run
answered diligently must end FINAL.

**Planted faults** (tests that must fail, and do):
- one lamport moved from the run to the executor inside a transaction
  (conserved in total, so only the per-account ledger sees it);
- a drift in the wait counter;
- a flipped ruling.

**History.** The first version checked conservation only in total, judged
rulings only on honest runs, and kept every probe 50 slots from its
deadline. An independent review found these and other blind spots
(reachability: `stage_grow`, retire, cancelled runs, reopened addresses). It
also found that a legitimate early `close_run` could be misread as a
failure. Version 2 answers each of them. Version 1 ran clean on 200
sequences, then on part of a 5,000-sequence campaign before it was stopped
for version 2. Two harness bugs were found and fixed on the way:
- the generator expected a refused "wrong executor" on moot, which takes no
  executor account;
- the oracle expected a challenger win on a dispute opened after another
  dispute had already won, which the program correctly rules moot.

Neither was a program bug.

## Not covered

- **Hello Graph is two steps and one output,** so descents are one level
  deep, there is one reveal cache, and output disputes start at the leaf.
  Deeper descents, list inputs and LX1 are covered by the oracle suites and
  the LX1 fuzz campaign (`lx1-fuzz-2026-10-04.md`), not by this fuzzer.
- **EDGE claims and STEP at the first step** have no oracle here. Their
  rulings are checked only by the soundness rules: an honest party never
  loses.
- **Staged opens (sub 27)** are LX1-only.
